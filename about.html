About us

One world.
Endless discoveries.

MODULORA is a developing English-language independent multi-topic media project focused on global events, emerging trends and evergreen information.

Editorial approach

Our planned publishing mix is roughly 50% timely content and 50% evergreen inspiration. Current news should be dated, supported by identifiable sources and distinguished from commentary. We aim to correct errors transparently.

Visual integrity

AI-created illustrative graphics must not be misrepresented as real documentary images of news events.

Contact

Questions or corrections can be raised via our project contact page. Do not post private information in public issues.
