Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy describes the current MODULORA informational website and our proposed owner-operated Pinterest content management integration. The Pinterest integration is not yet operating.

Website data

This static website currently has no visitor account system, forms, third-party advertisements or embedded analytics tracking. MODULORA does not intentionally collect visitor personal details through its code. GitHub Pages, our prospective hosting provider, may process request data under its own privacy terms.

Cookies and links

We currently do not install advertising cookies. Links to GitHub, Pinterest and other third parties lead to services governed by their own privacy policies.

Intended Pinterest integration

MODULORA Content Manager is intended to access an authorized owner-operated Pinterest Business account to prepare approved Pins, manage relevant boards and retrieve performance data. It is not intended to access unrelated accounts. Before activation, this policy will be updated to accurately describe data collected, storage, retention and deletion procedures.

Sharing and retention

The current static site does not run a user database or sell visitor data. Any hosting platform logs are controlled by the host.

Contact

Questions may be raised through the MODULORA repository contact page. Please do not disclose sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Changes

This policy will be updated when new features, such as analytics, advertising or active API workflows, are introduced.
